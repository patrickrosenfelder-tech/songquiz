# TuneDuel 🎵

**TuneDuel** is a real-time multiplayer music trivia game where players compete to identify songs from short audio clips. Test your music knowledge across genres, decades, and artists.

## What is TuneDuel?

TuneDuel lets you:
- Challenge friends or strangers to live song-recognition battles
- Race to identify songs from 5–15 second audio previews
- Score points for speed and accuracy
- Climb leaderboards across genre playlists and decades
